FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the things we get asked most.

Is SocialVire really free?
Yes. SocialVire is a 100% free Magento 2 extension. There are no paid tiers or hidden fees for the extension itself. Optional custom development work is quoted separately.
Which social platforms are supported?
WhatsApp, Facebook, X (Twitter), LinkedIn, Pinterest and Telegram, plus a Copy Link option. You can enable or disable each platform from the Admin Panel.
Where do I configure the extension?
Everything is managed from your Magento Admin Panel configuration settings, with no coding required. You can choose icon shape (circle or square), size (small, medium, large), floating position (left or right), enabled platforms and hover animation (scale, slide or glow).
What does the analytics feature track?
Each share is logged with the platform used, the product or page shared, the visitor's IP address and the date. You can review this in the share analytics grid and see a platform breakdown as a donut chart on your Admin Dashboard. Please read our Privacy Policy for details on your responsibilities as a store owner.
Where do the share buttons appear on my store?
A floating social sidebar appears on the side of your pages, and compact share buttons appear below the "Add to Cart" button on product pages.
Will it work with my Magento version or theme?
SocialVire is built for Magento 2. If you are unsure about your version, theme or a third-party module, email us your details and we will help you check compatibility.
Can you customize SocialVire for my store?
Absolutely. We can add features, adjust the design or integrate additional platforms. Write to info@webvire.com or call +91 7309277237 with your requirements.
