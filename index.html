For Customers

A Seamless Sharing Experience

Give your customers a fast, beautiful, and intuitive way to share your products.

Floating Social Sidebar

Social icons gracefully float on the side of every page, making it easy to share any product or page instantly.

Inline Product Sharing

Compact share buttons below the "Add to Cart" button let customers share specific products with one click.

Copy Link Feature

Customers can instantly copy the product URL to their clipboard with a visual confirmation alert.

For Administrators

Total Control from Your Admin Panel

No coding required. Configure every aspect of the sharing experience directly from Magento's configuration settings.

  • Shape & Size: Choose between Circle or Square, and Small, Medium, or Large icons.
  • Positioning: Place the floating icons on the Left or Right side of the screen.
  • Platform Selection: Enable or disable specific social platforms (WhatsApp, FB, X, etc.).
  • Hover Animations: Add engaging effects like Scale, Slide, or Glow on mouse hover.
