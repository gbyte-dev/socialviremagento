This Privacy Policy explains how Webvire Software ("Webvire", "we", "us") handles information in connection with SocialVire, our free social sharing and analytics extension for Magento 2, and this website. By using the extension or this website, you agree to the practices described below.

1Introduction

SocialVire adds floating social icons and product share buttons to Magento 2 stores and records share activity so store owners can see what is being shared. Two groups of people may be affected by this policy: store owners who install the extension, and shoppers who visit those stores and click share buttons.

2Information we collect

Through the extension (on a store using SocialVire)

When a visitor uses a share button, the extension records a share event containing:

Data Purpose
Social platform used (e.g. WhatsApp, Facebook, Copy Link) Show which platforms drive the most sharing
Product or page that was shared Show which products and pages are shared most
IP address of the visitor Share analytics and basic traffic insight
Date of the share Reporting over time

The extension does not ask visitors to create an account or enter personal details in order to share.

Through this website and our support channels

  • Information you choose to send us, such as your name, email address, phone number, store details and the content of your support or customization request.
  • Standard technical information that web servers receive automatically, such as IP address, browser type and pages visited.

3How we use information

  • To provide and operate the share analytics features inside the store owner's Magento Admin Panel.
  • To respond to support requests, installation questions and customization enquiries.
  • To maintain, secure and improve the extension and this website.
  • To meet legal obligations where applicable.

We do not sell personal information.

4Where data is stored

Share analytics are stored within the store owner's own Magento installation and database and are shown in that store's Admin Panel. Because the data lives on the store owner's systems, the store owner decides how long it is kept and who can access it.

In short: the share data collected by the extension belongs to the store that installed it. Webvire does not need access to it in order to provide the extension.

Information you send us directly by email, phone or other channels is kept only as long as needed to handle your request and for our legitimate business records.

5Third-party services

  • Social platforms. When a visitor clicks a share button, they are taken to that platform (such as WhatsApp, Facebook, X, LinkedIn, Pinterest or Telegram). Each platform handles information under its own privacy policy, which we do not control.
  • Google Fonts. This website loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address and browser details when the page loads.

6Store owner responsibilities

Because SocialVire records visitor IP addresses, which can be personal data under laws such as GDPR, store owners using the extension are responsible for:

  • Describing this collection in their own store's privacy policy and, where required, obtaining consent or providing notice.
  • Limiting access to analytics data and deleting it when it is no longer needed.
  • Complying with the data protection laws that apply to their store and customers.

7Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we handle. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and security of data inside a Magento store also depends on how that store is hosted and maintained by its owner.

8Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information held about you, or to object to or restrict certain processing.

  • For information you have sent to us, contact us using the details below and we will respond to your request.
  • For share analytics collected on a particular online store, please contact that store, as it controls the data.

9Children's privacy

SocialVire and this website are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

10Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last revised. Continued use of the extension or website after changes means you accept the updated policy.
